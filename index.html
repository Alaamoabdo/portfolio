About Me

I am Alaa, a Computer and Information Technology graduate focused on AI quality evaluation, conversational analysis, and thoughtful user experiences. I enjoy turning ambiguous interactions into clear, evidence-based judgments that help AI systems become more useful and reliable.

My background in web development gives me a strong technical foundation for understanding AI products, while my attention to detail helps me review nuanced responses, compare alternatives side by side, and identify unsupported claims, weak inferences, and unnatural personalization.

I bring strong written communication, structured reasoning, English proficiency, and independent remote-work habits. I am especially interested in evaluating grounding, integration, helpfulness, naturalness, and the quality of multi-turn prompt interactions, including safety compliance and red-teaming robustness.
