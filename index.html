About Me

I’m a Junior Cloud & DevOps Engineer based in Egypt, currently training with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative while completing my engineering degree. I help startups and small businesses make their application deployments more reliable, consistent, and easier to manage.

I provision AWS infrastructure with Terraform, configure and deploy applications with Ansible, containerize workloads with Docker and Kubernetes, and build CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins and Git.

What makes me different is my problem-solving approach. I don’t just follow a fixed process — I break problems down, find the root cause, and build practical, automated, and repeatable solutions.

I also value clear communication and documentation, so technical solutions are easier for clients to understand and manage.

My goal is to reduce repetitive manual work and help businesses build more consistent deployment processes while I continue growing through hands-on Cloud & DevOps projects.
